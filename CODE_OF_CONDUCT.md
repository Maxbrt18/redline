# Code of conduct

Redline is a small project. The rule is simple: treat people with respect, in issues,
pull requests and anywhere else you represent the project.

**Welcome:** being kind and patient, disagreeing about the work and not the person,
accepting a refusal, and assuming good faith.

**Not welcome:** harassment, insults, personal attacks, discrimination of any kind,
sexual language or attention, and publishing someone's private information.

Running and injuries are personal subjects. Do not post anyone's health data, including
your own, in a public issue.

**To report a problem,** open a
[private report](https://github.com/Maxbrt18/redline/security/advisories/new) rather
than a public issue. It will be read by the maintainer and handled discreetly.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, and block anyone who breaks these rules.
